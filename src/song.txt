About the Song:

"My All," released in 1998 as the fifth single from Mariah Carey's fifth studio album Butterfly, is a dramatic R&B and Latin-pop ballad driven by lush acoustic guitar arpeggios, subtle Latin percussion, and dynamic vocal arrangements. Co-written and co-produced with Walter Afanasieff, the track was inspired by a secret romantic encounter Carey had in Puerto Rico. Lyrically, it centers on intense longing, desperation, and emotional surrender, where the narrator declares a willingness to risk everything—giving up her life and soul—just to spend one more night held in the arms of a distant lover.
